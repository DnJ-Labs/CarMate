# CarMate

> NGE-BENGKEL JADI LEBIH MUDAH.

CarMate is a vehicle service booking platform that connects customers with automotive workshops through a mobile application and an admin CMS.

The platform helps users find workshops, manage their vehicles, schedule services, create bookings, track service progress in real time, and receive service reports. Workshop admins can manage workshops, bookings, and service processes through the CMS.

---

## Overview

Finding and managing vehicle maintenance can be inconvenient when booking is handled manually, communication is limited, and service progress is difficult to track.

CarMate provides a centralized platform that connects customers and workshops in one system.

With CarMate, users can:

- Find available workshops
- Manage their vehicles
- Choose service schedules
- Create service bookings
- Track booking and service progress
- Receive real-time notifications
- Access service reports
- View booking history

Workshop admins can manage their operational activities through the CarMate CMS.
